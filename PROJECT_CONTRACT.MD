## 1. HIGH-CONCEPT PITCH
A first-person POV 3D roguelike deckbuilder where the "deck" is a pair of customizable physical dice, a rigged craps felt, and a cybernetic/trinket-loaded left hand. Inspired by *Balatro* and *Buckshot Roulette*, the player sets their dice on the felt, throws them in POV into a hazard-filled craps pit, and watches a 3-layer synergy engine multiply chips into screen-shaking, synth-distorting combos—while manipulating physics and math to survive the dreaded **Seven-Out**.

---

## 2. THE CORE RUN LOOP ("SHOOTER'S RUN")
Each run is structured across **8 Antes (Tables)** with escalating monetary/chip quotas, culminating in **Pit Boss** encounters that apply table-wide physics or math debuffs.

1. **The Backroom (Shop Phase):** Spend winnings across four upgrade vendors (Bone Surgeon, Felt Architect, Pawn Broker, Table Modder).
2. **The Setup & Pre-Throw Ritual:**
   - The stickman pushes the dice back to the player across the black velvet felt.
   - The player leaves the dice on the felt and rotates them into a specific **Dice Set** (On-Felt Finger Tip, V-Set Slide, or Holographic Preset Snap).
   - The player places/adjusts chip stacks on the felt and activates any pre-throw Left-Hand abilities.
3. **The Come-Out Roll:** Establishes the **Point** (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) or scores immediate natural multipliers on 7 or 11 (and Craps on 2, 3, 12).
4. **The Point Phase (The Engine):** Every subsequent roll builds an unbanked combo pool and triggers placed bets, physical table hazards, and Left-Hand Trinkets.
   - **Cashing Out / Hitting the Point:** Hitting the established Point cashes out the multiplied pool with a massive Point Bonus and resets for a new Come-Out Roll.
   - **The Seven-Out (Failure State):** Rolling a sum of 7 during the Point Phase wipes the unbanked combo pool on the felt—unless intercepted by a Trinket, Insurance Chip, Left-Hand active ability, or Zero-Face math manipulation.

## 3. THE THREE-LAYER SYNERGY ENGINE & SCORING FORMULA

All throws resolve through a strict, sequential scoring equation:
$$\text{Total Payout} = (\text{Base Chips}) \times (\text{Throw Mult}) \times (\text{Bet Mult})$$

| Layer | Color Code | Trigger Phase | Sources & Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Base Chips** | **Cyan** | Mid-Roll & Settle | Generated by settled **Dice Face values**, special pip modifiers, and **Physical Collisions** (pinball bumpers, chip stack scatter, sticky puddles). |
| **2. Throw Mult** | **Magenta** | Mid-Air & Felt Travel | Accumulated before the dice stop: hitting the back alligator wall pyramids, passing through laser tripwires, landing inside the Target Ring, or hitting the rhythm release sweet spot. |
| **3. Bet Mult** | **Gold** | Post-Settle Queue | Triggered sequentially from left to right once dice settle: active **Felt Bets** (Pass, Don't Pass, Hardways, Field, Horn, Place, Even/Odd) followed by **Left-Hand Trinkets** and **Rail Props**. |

### 3.1 ECONOMY ARCHITECTURE: SCORE CHIPS VS. SPENDABLE CASH

To prevent exponential hyper-inflation (where a player banks 10,000 combo chips and spams 10,000 on the next roll), the economy employs a dual-currency roguelike architecture:

- **Option B (Implemented Core):**
  - **Score Chips (Ante Progress):** Generated by the Balatro equation $(\text{Base Chips} \times \text{Throw Mult} \times \text{Bet Mult})$. Stored in `UnbankedComboPool` and committed via manual banking (`[F]`) into `AnteProgress` to clear the Ante target quota.
  - **Spendable Bankroll ($ Cash):** Used to place table bets and purchase Backroom shop upgrades. When banking a winning hand, the player receives their returned wager principal plus base cash dividend (preventing combo multipliers from overflowing spendable cash). Cash is also replenished via Ante clear rewards, Point hit bounties (+$50 cash), and Gold Bullion dice faces.
  - **Progression Roguelite Loop:** Clearing an Ante awards a free upgrade draft choice between rounds.

- **Option A (Preserved Context - Table Wager Limits):**
  - *Context Note:* Evaluated alongside Option B. In Option A, all winnings remain unified spendable cash, but each Ante enforces strict casino **Table Maximum Wager Caps** (e.g. Ante 1 Max: $250; Ante 2 Max: $600) to clamp runaway bet sizing. Preserved in documentation for future exploration or high-roller mode.

## 4. TWO-HANDED POV ARCHITECTURE

Casino craps rules dictate one-handed throwing. The POV camera frames both hands with distinct mechanical roles:

### A. Right Hand (The Shooter — Active Throw Archetypes)
Controls on-felt dice setting and physical throwing. Swappable via character loadout or run upgrades:
1. **The Table-Skimmer (Shuffleboard/Curling Style):** Dice never leave the cloth. Pull fingers back and slide dice across the felt. Low vertical bounce, high horizontal momentum; plows through chip stacks and sticky puddles while preserving top-face stability.
2. **The Parabolic Lob (Helicopter/Soft Toss):** Scoops set dice off the felt and lobs them in a high trajectory arc over front-table hazards directly onto target zones or bumpers.
3. **The Back-Wall Whip (Fastball Flick):** High-velocity line drive straight into the back alligator wall for maximum bounce/collision multipliers and explosive scatter.
4. **The Mechanical Plunger (Cybernetic Spring Hand):** Pull-back spring plunger resting on the felt behind the dice. Delivers 100% repeatable velocity and straight-line trajectory.

### B. Left Hand (The Anchor — Passive "Jokers" & Active Cooldowns)
Rests on the padded leather rail on the bottom-left of the viewport. Features **7 Equipment Sockets**:
- **4 Ring Sockets** (Passive math/rule benders)
- **1 Wristwear Socket** (Clickable active cooldown abilities)
- **2 Rail Prop Sockets** (Ashtrays, cocktails, metronomes sitting beside the left hand)

## 5. INITIAL CONTENT DATABASE (V1 SCOPE)

### A. 15 Left-Hand Trinkets & Rail Props
1. **7-Segment LED Ring (Ring):** Odd roll totals grant `+4 Throw Mult`; Even roll totals grant `+30 Base Chips`.
2. **Mobius Band (Ring):** Horn Bets (2, 3, 11, 12) also trigger Field Bet multipliers twice.
3. **Magnetic Thumb Ring (Ring):** Applies physical attraction force pulling settling dice toward any chip stack with `$50+` value.
4. **Onyx Skull Ring (Ring):** Rolling a 7 no longer triggers a Seven-Out if the dice hit `>= 3` physical table hazards on that throw; destroys one random chip stack instead.
5. **Fibonacci Signet (Ring):** Rolling sequential totals across consecutive throws (e.g., 4 -> 5 -> 6) multiplies total Bet Mult by `x3`.
6. **Vampire Claws (Ring):** Whenever a Place Bet (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10) does not hit on a roll, drain 10% of its chip stack and permanently add `+1x Bet Mult` to the Pass Line.
7. **Casio Time-Splicer (Wristwear - Active):** *1 Charge/Table.* Click within 1.0s of a 7 settling to rewind physics by 2.0s and re-simulate with `+20%` velocity.
8. **Hydraulic Wrist-Brace (Wristwear - Active):** *2 Charges/Round.* Slam left fist on the rail while dice are settling to emit a physical impulse wave, flipping both dice 90 degrees toward the camera.
9. **Card Counter's Holoprojector (Wristwear - Passive UI):** Projects a real-time HUD readout of exact 7-Out probability based on current Dice Set and Target Ring radius.
10. **Synth-Cigar in Crystal Ashtray (Rail Prop):** Every 3rd throw, flicks glowing cyan ash onto a random Point box. Hitting that number grants `x2.5 Bet Mult`.
11. **Spilled Neon Margarita (Rail Prop):** Spawns a high-friction puddle on the center felt (`-70%` linear velocity, `+15 Base Chips` per 10cm slid).
12. **Rigged Metronome (Rail Prop):** Oscilliates on the rail. Releasing a throw on the exact beat guarantees both dice land on the same parity (both Even or both Odd).
13. **Shredded I.O.U. (Rail Prop):** Allows bankroll to drop to `-$500` without losing; grants `+1x Throw Mult` per `$50` of debt.
14. **Brass Knuckles Paperweight (Rail Prop):** Retains 50% of unbanked combo pool upon a Seven-Out.
15. **Mini Tesla Coil (Rail Prop):** Arcs lightning to the dice when they strike the back alligator wall, doubling all Base Chips on that throw.

### B. 8 Special Dice Faces (`UPDA_DiceFace`)
1. **Neon Wild Face (`?`):** Evaluates at settle time to whichever integer (1–6) yields the highest total payout (actively avoids making a sum of 7 unless playing a Don't Pass build).
2. **Splitter Face:** Upon settling face-up, spawns two miniature physics dice that bounce once and add their faces to the roll sum.
3. **Zero Face (`0`):** Evaluates as `0`. Pairing a Zero Face with a standard 1–6 die makes rolling a 7 mathematically impossible (`Max Sum = 6`).
4. **Double-Pip Face (`6/6`):** Counts as both `6` and `12` simultaneously for bet triggers and adds `+100 Base Chips`.
5. **Repulsor Face:** Cannot settle face-down. If bottom-facing when velocity drops near zero, fires an upward physics impulse to force another bounce.
6. **Vortex Face:** Pulls loose chips from adjacent betting zones into the winning bet's zone prior to payout calculation.
7. **Gold Bullion Face:** Evaluates as `0`, grants `+$15` directly to bankroll upon settling face-up or hitting the back wall.
8. **Glitch Face (`ERR`):** Randomizes value (`1–12`) on each physical bounce, adds `x2 Throw Mult`, and spikes CRT glitch intensity.
